# Sam (made-up interview)

## Getting started
[0:05] Interviewer: Thanks for doing this. How often do you work from cafés?
[0:11] Sam: Most weekdays, honestly. My flat is small and I get restless by ten.
[0:24] Interviewer: What made you start?
[0:27] Sam: The first lockdown ended and I just wanted noise around me.
It sounds odd, but silence makes me check my phone more.

## Choosing a place
[1:02] Interviewer: How do you pick a café?
[1:06] Sam: Plugs first. Then whether staff mind you staying three hours on one coffee.
[1:20] Sam: I keep a list in my notes app, ranked by how guilty they make me feel.
[1:34] Interviewer: Guilty?
[1:36] Sam: Yes. If they clear the table around me, I take the hint and leave.

## A typical day there
[2:10] Interviewer: What does a normal day look like?
[2:14] Sam: I buy something every two hours, like rent. A coffee, then a pastry, then another coffee.
[2:30] Sam: I don't even want the second coffee. It's the price of the chair.
[2:41] Interviewer: Do you ever take calls there?
[2:44] Sam: Short ones, outside. Taking a call at the table feels like bringing your office into someone's living room.

## Leaving
[3:20] Interviewer: When do you leave?
[3:23] Sam: When my laptop dies, usually. Or when I notice I'm the only one left without food.
