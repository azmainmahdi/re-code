# Tomás (made-up interview, no timestamps)

## Getting started
Interviewer: How often do you work from cafés?
Tomás: Twice a week, on the days my partner works from home. Our flat only has one table.
Interviewer: So it's about space?
Tomás: Space, and not hearing each other's meetings. We get on better since I started going out.

## Choosing a place
Interviewer: How do you pick a café?
Tomás: Somewhere I don't know the staff. If they know me, they chat, and then I feel rude putting headphones on.
I want to be a regular nowhere.

## A typical day there
Interviewer: What does a day there look like?
Tomás: Headphones on, no music playing. They're a sign that says don't talk to me.
I like the people around me, I just don't want to meet them.

## Leaving
Interviewer: How do you know it's time to go?
Tomás: When I've been there long enough that I start to feel watched. Usually around two hours.
Then I move to a second café for the afternoon. It resets the clock.
