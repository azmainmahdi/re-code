# Noor (made-up interview, no timestamps)

## Getting started
Interviewer: Where do you usually work?
Noor: A café near the station. I go there because nobody I know goes there.
Interviewer: Why does that matter?
Noor: At home I'm someone's daughter and someone's flatmate. At the café I'm just a laptop.
Nobody asks me to put the kettle on.

## Choosing a place
Interviewer: How do you pick a café?
Noor: It has to have a long table. I like sitting next to strangers who are also working.
It feels like an office I don't pay for.
Interviewer: What would make you stop going?
Noor: If they started playing loud music. A bit of chatter is fine, but music with words, no.

## A typical day there
Interviewer: Walk me through a normal day at the café.
Noor: I get there at nine, order a flat white, and do my hardest work before lunch.
After lunch I switch to emails, because by then it's busy and I can't think deeply anyway.
Interviewer: Do you talk to anyone?
Noor: Not really. There's a man who nods at me every morning. We've never spoken. I'd miss him if he stopped coming.

## Leaving
Interviewer: How do you decide when to leave?
Noor: When the lunch crowd comes in and someone is standing with a tray, looking for a seat.
I feel like I'm taking something from them.
