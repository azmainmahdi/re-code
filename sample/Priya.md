# Priya (made-up interview)

## Getting started
[0:04] Interviewer: How did you start working from cafés?
[0:08] Priya: My company closed our office last year. They gave us a coffee allowance instead.
[0:19] Priya: So the café isn't really my choice. It's where the allowance works.
[0:30] Interviewer: How do you feel about that?
[0:33] Priya: Mixed. I miss having a desk that is mine, with my own mug on it.

## Choosing a place
[1:05] Interviewer: How do you pick a café?
[1:09] Priya: It has to take the company card, so that cuts it down to three.
[1:18] Priya: Out of those, I go to the one with a corner seat. My back to the wall, screen facing it, so no one can read my emails.

## A typical day there
[1:52] Interviewer: What is a normal day like?
[1:55] Priya: I have video calls most mornings. I whisper, and I hate it.
[2:06] Priya: The barista knows my order now. That's nice. It's the closest thing I have to a colleague.

## Leaving
[2:40] Interviewer: When do you leave?
[2:43] Priya: At four, before the school crowd. Once the noise goes up, I can't hear my calls.
[2:55] Priya: I still feel like a guest who has stayed too long, even though the company is paying.
